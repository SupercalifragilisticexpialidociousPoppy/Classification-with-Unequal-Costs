\section{Code listing and contribution statement}
\label{sec:contrib}
\paragraph{Member 4.} Derived the T4 scoring-rule proofs, calibration decomposition and
temperature example in Section~\ref{sec:calibration}. Implemented the C6 held-out
inspection experiment in \path{project4/experiments/task5_inspection.py}, using the Gaussian
classifier by Member 3, logistic regression by Member 2, and analytic reference and shared
infrastructure by Member 6. Section~\ref{sec:c6} reports the generated results.
The assigned review of Member 5's C4 implementation is pending availability of that code.
% Other members' contributions belong here.

\paragraph{Member 5.} Derived the T5 prior-shift correction in Section~\ref{sec:t5} (posterior-odds
correction, its form for logistic regression and for the Gaussian model, and the equivalent shifted thresholds)
with its figure, \path{project4/experiments/t5_prior_correction.py}, and the functions in
\path{project4/prior_shift.py} that the C5 experiments use. Implemented the C4 forecast-quality measures in
\path{project4/calibration.py} (log-loss, Brier score, reliability bins, ECE, temperature and Platt scaling)
and the Task 3 experiment in \path{project4/experiments/task3_calibration.py}, with
\path{tests/test_calibration.py} and \path{tests/test_prior_shift.py}, using the classifiers by Members 2 and 3
and the shared infrastructure by Member 6. Section~\ref{sec:c4} reports the results.
