\section{Code listing and contribution statement}
\label{sec:contrib}
\paragraph{Member 1.} Derived the T1 decision theory in Section~\ref{sec:decision} (loss matrix,
Bayes action for a general loss matrix, reject thresholds, imperfect inspection, equal-cost check) with its
figure, \path{project4/experiments/t1_expected_loss.py}, and wrote the introduction (Section~\ref{sec:intro}).
Implemented the C5 prior-shift experiments in \path{project4/experiments/task4_prior_shift.py} with
\path{tests/test_c5_prior_shift.py}, using the correction by Member 5, the classifiers by Members 2 and 3 and
the shared infrastructure by Member 6. Section~\ref{sec:c5} reports the results. Reviewed Member 6's C1
infrastructure against the T1 derivation.

\paragraph{Member 4.} Derived the T4 scoring-rule proofs, calibration decomposition and
temperature example in Section~\ref{sec:calibration}. Implemented the C6 held-out
inspection experiment in \path{project4/experiments/task5_inspection.py}, using the Gaussian
classifier by Member 3, logistic regression by Member 2, and analytic reference and shared
infrastructure by Member 6. Section~\ref{sec:c6} reports the generated results.
The assigned review of Member 5's C4 implementation is pending availability of that code.

\paragraph{Member 5.} Derived the T5 prior-shift correction (Section~\ref{sec:t5}) and wrote
\path{project4/prior_shift.py}, used by the C5 experiments. Implemented the C4 measures in
\path{project4/calibration.py} and the Task 3 experiment \path{project4/experiments/task3_calibration.py},
with tests, using the classifiers by Members 2 and 3 and the infrastructure by Member 6
(Section~\ref{sec:c4}).
